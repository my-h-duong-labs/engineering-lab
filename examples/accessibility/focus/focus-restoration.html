Focus restoration

Use the keyboard: press Enter or Space on Open dialog, then try Tab, Shift+Tab, and Escape, and close it with either button. Opened this way, focus returns to Open dialog when the dialog closes. If you open it with a mouse, browsers differ: Safari does not focus a button when it is clicked, so there may be nothing to return to. The status line below reports what happened.
